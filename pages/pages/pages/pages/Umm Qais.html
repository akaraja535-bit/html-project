Umm Qais: Gadara

Umm Qais, historically known as Gadara, is a city of incredible contrasts. It was once a flourishing center for arts, culture, and science within the Decapolis, a union of ten major Greco-Roman cities.

The site is unique for its collection of ruins built from black basalt stone, which stands out against the lush green landscape of the north.

Key Highlights:

  1. The Roman Theaters: Gadara features both a western and a northern theater, showcasing the city's once-thriving cultural life.
  2. The Cardo Maximus: Walk along the ancient colonnaded main street that still holds its original paving stones.
  3. Views of the Sea of Galilee: The site offers arguably the most breathtaking view of the Sea of Galilee and the Golan Heights.
  4. Ottoman-Era Houses: The village area near the ruins displays beautifully preserved stone houses from the Ottoman period.
