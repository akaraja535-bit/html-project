Aqaba: The Red Sea Gateway

Aqaba is Jordan's only coastal city, located on the northern tip of the Red Sea. It is a vibrant destination known for its warm, crystalline waters, lively atmosphere, and historical significance.

Beyond its beaches, Aqaba serves as a gateway to the desert of Wadi Rum and the ancient city of Petra, making it a perfect base for a Jordanian tour.

Key Highlights:

  1. Diving and Snorkeling: The Red Sea here is home to hundreds of species of corals and fish, making it a world-class diving destination.
  2. Aqaba Marine Park: Dedicated to preserving the local marine ecosystem.
  3. Ayla Ruins: Explore the remains of the early Islamic city of Ayla.
  4. Local Cuisine: Enjoy fresh, locally caught seafood and authentic Jordanian dishes in the city center.
