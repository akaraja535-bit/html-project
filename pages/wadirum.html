Wadi Rum: Valley of the Moon

Wadi Rum, also known as the "Valley of the Moon," is a vast, silent landscape of ancient riverbeds and towering granite and sandstone mountains. It is a place of profound beauty and desert tranquility, listed as a UNESCO World Heritage site.

The area has been inhabited by many cultures, including the Nabataeans, who left their mark in the form of rock paintings and temples.

Key Highlights:

  1. 4x4 Desert Safaris: The best way to traverse the deep red sands and see natural arches and rock inscriptions.
  2. Bedouin Hospitality: Stay in traditional "glamping" camps, enjoy authentic Bedouin cuisine like Zarb (underground roasted meat), and listen to stories by the fire.
  3. Stargazing: With minimal light pollution, the desert sky provides one of the clearest views of the Milky Way.
  4. Film Locations: Wadi Rum has served as the backdrop for many sci-fi movies, including The Martian and Dune.
