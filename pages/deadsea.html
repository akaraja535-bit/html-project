The Dead Sea

At 430 meters below sea level, the Dead Sea is the lowest point on the face of the Earth. Its shores are one of the most spectacular natural and spiritual landscapes in the world.

The water contains a unique concentration of salts and minerals, providing therapeutic benefits that have attracted visitors for thousands of years, including Herod the Great and Queen Cleopatra.

Key Highlights:

  1. Effortless Floating: The high salinity allows you to float on the water surface without any effort.
  2. Healing Mineral Mud: The black mud found on the shores is rich in minerals and is renowned for its skin-healing properties.
  3. Wellness Resorts: Numerous high-end spas offer treatments utilizing the natural resources of the sea.
  4. Nearby Sites: Mount Nebo and the Baptism Site (Bethany Beyond the Jordan) are just a short drive away.
