Title: A Journey Through Time: From the Magic of Petra to the Serenity of Wadi Rum

Featured Destinations

Petra:

Explore the iconic Rose Red City, one of the New Seven Wonders of the World. Walk through the Siq to marvel at the Treasury (Al-Khazneh) and hike up to the Monastery (Ad-Deir).

Wadi Rum:

Experience the "Valley of the Moon" through 4x4 jeep tours across sandstone mountains, stargazing under clear desert skies, and staying in traditional Bedouin camps.

The Dead Sea:

Float effortlessly in the lowest point on Earth and enjoy a relaxing natural spa treatment using mineral-rich mud.

Aqaba:

Jordan's coastal haven offers a tropical contrast to the northern highlands.

  • Dive or snorkel among vibrant coral reefs and marine life in the Gulf of Aqaba.
  • Relax on sandy public or private beaches along the Red Sea shore.
  • Enjoy coastal dining and evening strolls through lively markets.
Umm Qais:

An ancient Greco-Roman hill town known historical Gadara, positioned in the far northwest.

  • Walk through striking black basalt ruins, colonnaded streets, and ancient theaters.
  • Take in panoramic views overlooking the Sea of Galilee and the Golan Heights.
  • Sample local farm-to-table cuisine prepared by northern local kitchens.
Ajloun Castle:

A prominent 12th-century Ayyubid military fortress built to defend against Crusaders.

  • Explore ancient stone halls, towers, and defensive battlements overlooking lush oak forests.
  • Enjoy sweeping views across the Ajloun valley and northern countryside.
